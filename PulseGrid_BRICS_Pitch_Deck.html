The Problem

Grassroots Healthcare Supply Chains Suffer From Fatal Blind Spots

Zero Real-Time Visibility

Central ministries cannot track daily medicine burn, bed availability, or doctor attendance across tens of thousands of rural clinics until paper audits arrive weeks later.

Fatal Emergency Stock-Outs

During seasonal dengue spikes, floods, or snakebite surges, remote clinics run out of antivenom, IV fluids, and insulin within 24 hours while district warehouses 50km away have idle surplus.

Sovereignty & Data Silos

Developing nations share identical subtropical disease vectors, but strict privacy regulations (DPDP, LGPD, POPIA) prevent raw patient data sharing between nations.

The Solution

PulseGrid BRICS: The Autonomous Resilient Health Grid

1. Real-Time PHC Telemetry

Continuous visibility into medicine runways, ICU and maternity bed occupancy, and medical staff attendance across the entire national PHC grid.

2. Automated Redistribution

Algorithmic solver matching deficit clinics with nearest surplus depots, generating authorized transfer manifests with vehicle specifications in <3 hours.

3. Gemini 3.8 Flash Triage Engine

Sub-second agentic reasoning for emergency triage briefs, incident dispatch orders, and predictive surge anomalies tuned with Google AI Studio system instructions.

4. BRICS Sovereign Federated Mesh

Federated Averaging (DP-FedAvg) allowing India, Brazil, South Africa, and Egypt to share outbreak prediction weights with zero citizen data leaving national borders.
