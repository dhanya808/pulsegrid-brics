The Grassroots Challenge

Public Healthcare Supply Chains Fail Where They Matter Most

Blind-Spot Disconnect

Central health ministries lack real-time visibility into grassroots PHCs and sub-centres. Stock telemetry takes weeks to aggregate through paper logs.

Fatal Emergency Stock-Outs

When seasonal floods, dengue spikes, or snakebites surge, local clinics exhaust critical antivenom, IV fluids, and insulin within 24 hours while nearby depots sit idle.

Sovereignty & Data Silos

Global South nations share identical tropical epidemiological vectors, yet privacy and sovereignty barriers prevent collaborative predictive modeling.

The Innovation

PulseGrid BRICS: The Intelligent Autonomous Health Grid

National Real-Time Telemetry

Continuous tracking of essential medicines (runway days), ICU/maternity bed occupancy, and medical doctor/nurse shift attendance across national PHC networks.

Autonomous Cross-District Redistribution

Algorithmic priority pairing that transfers surplus stocks to deficit clinics in <3 hours, ensuring cold-chain integrity and vehicle routing without exhausting donor resilience.

Gemini 3.8 Flash Triage Engine

Instantaneous agentic reasoning for emergency stock-out triage, surge demand prediction, and automated transfer manifest authoring with fine-tuned system instructions.

BRICS Sovereign Federated Mesh

Differentially private collaborative learning (DP-FedAvg). India, Brazil, South Africa, and Egypt share epidemiological surge vectors with 0 raw citizen records exported.
